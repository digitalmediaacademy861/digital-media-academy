How to spot a fake scholarship

  • A real scholarship or grant never asks you to pay to apply or to "release" your money.
  • Only apply through the organisation's official website. Be careful with links sent on WhatsApp or social media.
  • Never share your bank PIN, card details or passwords.
  • If an offer promises guaranteed money with no application, it is almost certainly a scam.
