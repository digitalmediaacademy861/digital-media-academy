FAQ

Questions people ask

Is the Video Editing training free?

Yes. The Video Editing training is free to join.

Do I need any experience?

No. The training is beginner-friendly and starts from the basics.

How do I join?

Tap "Join free training", then tap Register and fill in the short form. When you submit it, the WhatsApp learning group opens.

Where do the lessons take place?

Inside the WhatsApp learning group, where you'll also get updates about the programme.

When will the other courses open?

Graphic Design, Social Media Management and the Online Surveys course are coming soon. Check back here for updates.

Who runs the Academy?

Godswill Udo (Founder) and Daniel Usodo (Co-Founder), both students at Akwa Ibom State University.

I have another question. Who do I ask?

Message support on WhatsApp using the green button on this page.
