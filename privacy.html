Privacy Policy

What information we collect, why, and how we look after it.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information Digital Media Academy collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and we only collect what we need.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address when you sign up, or the name and email from your Google account if you sign in with Google. Your password is handled by Google Firebase and we cannot see it.
  • Payment records: when you pay, we keep your name, email, the amount, the date and your payment reference. Your card or bank details go to Paystack. We never see or store them.
  • Course access: a record that your account has paid, so your lessons stay open.
  • Certificate names: if you use the certificate page, the name you type is used only in your browser to make the certificate.
  • Messages: anything you send us on WhatsApp.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account and let you log in.
  • To confirm your payment and open your lessons.
  • To keep records of payments and answer questions or problems.
  • To stop fraud and misuse of the website.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to send you adverts from other companies.

4. Who else handles your information

We use trusted services to run the website. They process information for us under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: accounts and login.
  • Paystack: payments.
  • Cloudflare: the service that confirms payments and stores who has paid.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the website.
  • Google Drive and Google Fonts: lesson videos and the text styles on the pages.
  • WhatsApp: support messages.

Some of these companies are based outside Nigeria, so your information may be processed in other countries.

5. Information kept on your device

The website keeps a login session in your browser so you stay signed in, and a saved copy of pages so they load faster. We do not use advertising cookies. You can clear this in your browser settings, and you will simply be asked to log in again.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account for as long as you use it. We keep payment records for as long as needed for accounting, disputes and the law. You can ask us to delete your account, and we will do so unless we must keep certain records.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also object to how we use it. Message us on WhatsApp and we will respond. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Keeping it safe

We use trusted, secure services, and payments are checked on a secure server. No website can promise perfect security, so please keep your password private and use one you do not use anywhere else.

9. Children

This website is meant for adults and for young people with a parent or guardian's permission. If you think a child has given us information without permission, tell us and we will remove it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

11. Contact

Message us on WhatsApp using the Support link. We reply as soon as we can.
