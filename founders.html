The people behind the Academy

Digital Media Academy is built around a simple idea: learn something useful, practise it, and keep growing.

Godswill Udo

Founder

Godswill is a Nigerian student of History and International Studies at Akwa Ibom State University. He is a digital media professional and creative entrepreneur with skills in mobile photography, videography, video editing and social media management.

He cares about leadership, international relations, technology and creativity, and wants to empower young people with valuable digital skills.

Daniel Usodo

Co-Founder

Daniel studies History and International Studies at Akwa Ibom State University. His interests include diplomatic and strategic studies, documentary production, multimedia content, public speaking and public relations.

As Co-Founder, he is passionate about digital innovation and creative storytelling, and about helping young people gain practical skills for a changing digital world.
