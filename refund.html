Refund Policy

When you can get your money back and how to ask.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

The short version

The Video Editing lessons open to you as soon as you pay, so we do not refund a payment just because you changed your mind after using the lessons. We do fix mistakes quickly and refund you where something went wrong on our side.

When you will get a refund

  • You were charged twice for the same course. We refund the extra payment.
  • You paid but the lessons never opened, and we could not fix it within 3 days.
  • The lessons are not available and we cannot give you access.
  • A payment was taken by mistake or without your permission. Tell us right away.

When we cannot refund

  • You have opened and used the lessons and simply changed your mind.
  • Your access was switched off because the lessons or links were shared, copied or resold (see our Terms).
  • You expected a result, such as a job or income, that we never promised.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of your payment.
  2. Send the email you used to pay and your payment reference. Your reference starts with DMA_ and is in the receipt Paystack emailed you.
  3. Tell us what went wrong.

We will check your payment and reply as soon as we can.

How you get the money

Approved refunds go back to the same card or bank account you paid with, through Paystack. It usually takes 5 to 10 working days to show, depending on your bank.

Contact

Message us on WhatsApp using the Support link. We reply as soon as we can.
